A human voice from code, the old way

This is formant synthesis, the technique behind speech machines since the 1970s. A buzzing tone stands in for the vocal cords. Three band-pass filters stand in for the throat and mouth; moving their centre frequencies changes the vowel.

VowelF1 (Hz)F2 (Hz)F3 (Hz)Mouth shape

Formant values are averages for adult male speakers (Peterson & Barney, 1952). Hear how robotic it sounds: modern natural voices come from neural models trained on recordings, not from hand-written formulas.
